What the roofing work may involve

The work depends on the condition and type of damage. Structural repair addresses the roof’s stability. Shingle replacement involves removing old or damaged shingles and changing them out. Storm damage repair restores roof areas affected by a storm, while emergency hail damage repair addresses impact damage from hailstones.

Before work starts, use this short checklist:

  • Identify the concern and show any photos you took safely from the ground.
  • Ask which repair service is being recommended and why.
  • Confirm the areas and tasks included in the proposed work.
  • Ask how you should prepare the area around your home.

Avoid assuming that a few visibly damaged shingles are the only concern, or that every roof issue calls for a full replacement. Ask for an explanation of the recommended scope.
